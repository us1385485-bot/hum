Our Journey

Milestones that shaped Ideal Sports into what it is today.

2016
The First Kick-Off

Ideal Sports launches as a passion project — a simple blog covering weekend match analysis for a small community of fans.

2018
Going Live

We introduce real-time live scores and match tracking, growing our user base to 100K monthly visitors within the first year.

2020
Global Expansion

Partnerships with 30+ clubs across Europe and South America bring exclusive insights and behind-the-scenes content to our platform.

2022
The Mobile Revolution

A complete redesign delivers a lightning-fast, mobile-first experience, pushing us past 1 million monthly active users.

2024
Data-Driven Insights

Advanced analytics and player tracking tools launch, giving fans and clubs deeper tactical intelligence than ever before.

2026
The Ideal Era

Today, Ideal Sports serves 2.4M+ fans monthly across 12 leagues, partnering with 96 clubs and 40+ stadiums worldwide.
